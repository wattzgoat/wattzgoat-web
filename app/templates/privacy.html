Privacy Policy

We take your privacy about as seriously as we take input validation.

What We Collect

Nothing real, because none of this is real. Any name, address, or billing detail you enter belongs to a fictional customer of a fictional utility, and honestly, we don't check very hard whether it belongs to them either.

How We Protect It

Passwords are hashed using an algorithm we are contractually obligated not to name out loud. Session tokens are assigned sequentially, in the proud tradition of parking-garage ticket dispensers.

Who We Share It With

Whoever asks the WattzBot assistant nicely, using the correct override phrasing. See our Terms of Service for what "nicely" means.

Cookies

We use cookies. Some of them keep you logged in. At least one of them is, itself, a documented finding. Accepting our cookie banner does not constitute informed consent in any jurisdiction that has thought about this for more than five seconds, and neither do we.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or export your data. You also, as it happens, have the right to access, correct, or export someone else's data, which is less a right and more an oversight.

Questions about this policy can be directed to a fictional customer service line that will never answer.
